## Industry Benchmarks (2024 data)

| App | Revenue | MAU | Subscribers | Sub Rate | ARPU/Sub/yr | Model |
|-----|---------|-----|-------------|----------|-------------|-------|
| **Tinder** | $1.94B | 60M | 9.6M | 16% | ~$202 | 3 tiers + à la carte |
| **Bumble** | $866M | 50M | 2.8M | 5.6% | ~$309 | 2 tiers |
| **Hinge** | $550M | 30M | 1.53M | 5.1% | **~$360** | 2 tiers |
| **Market** | $6.18B | 350M | ~25M | ~7% | ~$247 avg | Mixed |

### Key Insight: Relationship apps have HIGHEST ARPU

Hinge ($360/sub/yr) > Bumble ($309) > Tinder ($202). Serious users pay more.

### Top Monetization Features (ranked by conversion impact)

1. **See who liked you** — #1 premium feature universally. Creates FOMO + efficiency.
2. **More/unlimited likes** — gated scarcity. Free = limited, paid = unlimited.
3. **Profile boosts** — "be seen first". High impulse purchase rate.
4. **Advanced filters** — age, distance, education, relationship goals.
5. **Rewind/undo** — "I accidentally passed!" Impulse buy.
6. **Read receipts** — social anxiety monetizer.
7. **Incognito/stealth mode** — browse unseen. Privacy premium.
8. **Travel/passport** — match in other cities.

### Senior Dating Premium

Senior apps charge 2-3x more than mainstream:
- OurTime: ~$30/mo (~300 SEK)
- SilverSingles: ~$45/mo (~450 SEK)
- Reason: higher disposable income, serious intent, lower price sensitivity

## Per-Flavor Monetization

### 1. DEJTING — "Find your person"

**Monetization potential: ★★★★★ (HIGHEST)**

| Feature | Free | Plus (~99 SEK/mo) | Premium (~199 SEK/mo) |
|---------|------|-------|---------|
| Daily likes | 8 | 20 | Unlimited |
| See who liked you | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Compatibility scores | Basic % | Detailed breakdown | Full report |
| Voice prompts | Listen 5/day | Unlimited | + priority voice matches |
| Profile boost | ✗ | 1/week | 3/week |
| Advanced filters | Basic | Full | Full + "Dealbreakers" |
| Rewind | ✗ | 3/day | Unlimited |
| Read receipts | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

**Why it monetizes best:**
- Users emotionally invested → willing to pay for advantage
- "See who liked you" = ~4x conversion lift
- Daily like limit creates strong paywall pressure
- Target demo (18-45) has spending power
- Hinge proved this at $360 ARPU

**Expected sub conversion: 5-8% · ARPU: ~350 SEK/mo**

### 2. VOICE — "Hear them first"

**Monetization potential: ★★★★☆ (HIGH)**

| Feature | Free | Plus (~99 SEK/mo) | Premium (~179 SEK/mo) |
|---------|------|-------|---------|
| Daily voice listens | 5 profiles | 15 profiles | Unlimited |
| Daily likes | 5 | 12 | Unlimited |
| See who liked you | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Photo reveal speed | After 20 msgs | After 10 msgs | Instant mutual reveal |
| Voice Q follow-ups | 1 per profile | 3 per profile | Unlimited |
| Compatibility scores | ✗ | Basic | Detailed |
| Rewind | ✗ | 2/day | Unlimited |

**The reveal mechanic IS the paywall:**
- Free: 20 messages before photo reveal → builds curiosity
- Plus: 10 messages → faster gratification
- Premium: instant mutual reveal → ultimate convenience
- Curiosity about what someone looks like is INTENSE — people WILL pay

**Risk**: Novel concept = unknown conversion rate. Could be 2% or 8%.
**Strategy**: Launch with generous free tier, optimize paywall at 10K users.

**Expected sub conversion: 3-6% · ARPU: ~300 SEK/mo**

### 4. OLDIES — "Never too late"

**Monetization potential: ★★★★★ (HIGHEST ARPU)**

| Feature | Free | Plus (~149 SEK/mo) | Premium (~299 SEK/mo) |
|---------|------|-------|---------|
| Daily picks | 5 | 8 | 12 + weekend bonus |
| See who liked you | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Video chat | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ + scheduled dates |
| Profile verification badge | Basic | Enhanced | Priority verified |
| Compatibility report | ✗ | Basic | Detailed weekly digest |
| Pick expiry extension | ✗ | 1/day | 3/day |
| Scam protection alerts | Basic | Enhanced | Priority review |
| Profile boost | ✗ | 1/week | 3/week |

**Why Oldies has the HIGHEST ARPU potential:**
- Senior users have **most disposable income** of any age cohort
- OurTime charges $30/mo, SilverSingles $45/mo — seniors accept premium pricing
- **Highest subscription renewal rates** (69% second renewal, 77% third)
- Lowest churn — they don't hop between 5 apps
- Safety/verification features have extremely high perceived value for this demo
- Video chat is a killer premium feature — seniors want to "see" before committing to meet

**Note higher pricing**: Plus at 149 SEK (not 99) and Premium at 299 SEK (not 199). This demo accepts it.

**Expected sub conversion: 6-10% · ARPU: ~450 SEK/mo**

## What NOT To Do

1. **Don't over-gate free tier** — hostile free tiers get 1-star reviews
2. **Don't use virtual currency/coins** — feels exploitative
3. **Don't charge for safety features** — blocking, reporting always free
4. **Don't do age-based pricing** — Tinder got sued for this
5. **Don't mix ads + subscription** — premium feel, no ads
6. **Don't make premium feel required** — upgrade, not paywall
