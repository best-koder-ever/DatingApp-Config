## Why Niche Matters

Generic dating app ("singles in your area"):
- 2-5% free→paid conversion
- $0.40-1.00 revenue per user
- Competing with Tinder's $8B marketing budget

Niche dating app (specific painful situation):
- 12-20% free→paid conversion
- $3.60-4.50 revenue per user  
- **4x revenue, 1/100th the competition**

## Niche Options Analysis

### Option 1: Nyinflyttade i stad (New to City, 25-35)

**Market Size:**
- Sweden: ~150k people move to new city annually (25-35 age)
- Stockholm alone: ~30k/year new residents
- **Addressable**: Large enough to build sustainable business

**Pain Intensity (1-10): 9/10**
- Acute loneliness in first 3-6 months
- No social network, perfect time to meet people
- Fear: "If I don't make friends now, I'll settle into isolation"

**Urgency Triggers:**
- Time-based: "People who moved <3 months ago" (similar life stage)
- Geographic: "5 people in your neighborhood just moved here"
- Event-based: "Weekend matches — meet before Monday loneliness"

**Hook for Marketing:**
> "Just moved to Stockholm? Meet others who arrived in the last 6 months.  
> No awkward small talk — everyone's new, everyone gets it."

**Paywall Moment:**
```
"[Name] moved to Södermalm 2 weeks ago (just like you!).  
They want to grab fika this weekend.  
💬 Read their message — 1 Spark or upgrade to Premium"
```

**Why This Wins:**
- ✅ Large market size (scalable)
- ✅ High pain (loneliness + urgency to act fast)  
- ✅ Clear targeting (Facebook ads: "Moved to Stockholm?")  
- ✅ Low competition (no major app for this niche)
- ✅ International expansion (works in any city globally)

**Risks:**
- Churn: If they make friends, leave app (Mitigation: "Also find dates, not just friends")
- Geographic concentration (Mitigation: Start Stockholm/Göteborg/Malmö only)

### Option 2: Föräldrar varannan vecka (Shared Custody, 30-45)

**Market Size:**
- Sweden: ~200k single parents with shared custody
- 30-45 age range: ~80k
- **Addressable**: Medium — enough to start, may need expansion

**Pain Intensity: 10/10**
- Extreme loneliness every other week when kids gone
- Need partner who understands "I can't meet this week, I have the kids"
- Fear: "I'll never find someone who gets my situation"

**Urgency Triggers:**
- Calendar-based: "You're kid-free this weekend — 8 matches are also free"
- Scarcity: "Only 23 single parents in [area] with same custody schedule"
- FOMO: "5 matches expire Monday when kids come back"

**Hook for Marketing:**
> "Date other single parents. We only show you matches when you're BOTH kid-free.  
> No explaining, no guilt — everyone gets it."

**Paywall Moment:**
```
⏰ "This weekend only: 7 matches are kid-free (just like you)  
[Name] wants to meet Saturday for drinks — confirm now  
Premium: $9.99 for this weekend only (cancel anytime)"
```

**Why This Wins:**
- ✅ **Highest pain intensity** (loneliness + time constraints + need for empathy)
- ✅ Built-in urgency (kid-free weekends are finite windows)
- ✅ High willingness to pay (older demographic, desperate for understanding partner)
- ✅ Unique mechanic (custody calendar sync = no other app does this)

**Risks:**
- Smaller market size (Mitigation: Expand to "all single parents" after proving concept)
- Complex onboarding (need custody schedule input)

### Option 3: Akademiker i småstäder (Educated in Small Towns)

**Market Size:**
- Sweden small towns (<100k population): ~3M people
- University-educated 25-50: ~400k
- **Addressable**: Medium — concentrated in specific towns

**Pain Intensity: 8/10**
- Limited dating pool (most educated people left for cities)
- Fear of settling for incompatible match due to scarcity
- Isolation: "No one here gets me"

**Urgency Triggers:**
- Scarcity: "Only 17 active users in Västerås this month"
- Competition: "3 people viewed [Name]'s profile today — match before they do"
- New user alerts: "2 new university-educated users joined this week"

**Hook for Marketing:**
> "Living in [small town] with a degree?  
> Connect with the 23 other university-educated singles here."

**Paywall Moment:**
```
🔴 Only 12 active users in Västerås  
[Name] (Master's in Engineering) wants to chat  
See all 12 before someone else matches them — Premium $24.99/mo
```

**Why This Wins:**
- ✅ Extreme scarcity = high urgency
- ✅ Clear targeting (LinkedIn ads to people in small towns)
- ✅ Underserved market (Tinder doesn't work well in small populations)

**Risks:**
- Limited growth potential (can't scale beyond small towns)
- Geographic fragmentation (need critical mass per town = slow)

### Option 4: Nyskilda (Newly Divorced, 35-50)

**Market Size:**
- Sweden: ~25k divorces/year
- 35-50 age range: ~15k/year
- **Addressable**: Medium — steady new supply

**Pain Intensity: 8/10**
- Haven't dated in 10-15 years, fear of rejection
- Rusty social skills, need "safe" space to practice
- Emotional: "I'm starting over"

**Urgency Triggers:**
- Similarity: "Match with people who got divorced 1-3 months ago (like you)"
- Empathy: "No judgment — everyone here is newly single"
- Milestone-based: "It's been 90 days since your divorce — time to try dating?"

**Hook for Marketing:**
> "Newly divorced? Date others who just got out of long-term relationships.  
> Everyone's rusty, everyone's nervous — find your confidence again."

**Paywall Moment:**
```
💙 [Name] got divorced 2 months ago (just like you)  
Their message: "I'm nervous too, but want to try..."  
Read full message — 1 Spark
```

**Why This Wins:**
- ✅ High empathy (reduces fear of judgment)
- ✅ Continuous supply (divorces happen every month)
- ✅ Older demographic = higher income = more willingness to pay

**Risks:**
- Emotional baggage (users may not be ready to date = high churn)
- Negative framing ("divorced" = stigma in some cultures)

## Recommendation: Start with Option 1 (New to City)

### Why "New to City" is the Best First Niche:

1. **Largest Market**: 150k/year in Sweden, millions globally (can expand to any city)
2. **Positive Framing**: "New adventure" vs "divorced" or "lonely parent"
3. **High Urgency**: 3-6 month window before people settle into friend groups
4. **Clear Targeting**: Facebook/Instagram ads to people who just moved
5. **International**: Test Stockholm, then expand to London, Berlin, NYC

### Implementation Plan:

**Phase 1: Onboarding (Week 1)**
- Add question: "When did you move to [city]?"
  - Options: <1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1+ year
- Filter: Only show matches who answered <6 months OR who want to meet new arrivals

**Phase 2: First Paywall (Week 2)**
- Free: See 3 matches/day who also moved recently
- Paywall: Get match → see preview → **can't read message without 1 Spark**
- Notification: "[Name] moved to [neighborhood] 3 weeks ago and wants to meet this weekend"

**Phase 3: Launch (Week 3)**
- Facebook ads targeting: 
  - Location: Stockholm, Göteborg, Malmö
  - Demographics: Moved to city in last 6 months (Facebook knows this from location history)
  - Age: 25-35
- Measure: Free→Spark conversion (target 20%+)

**Phase 4: Iterate (Week 4)**
- If conversion <15%: Test different paywall moments  
- If conversion >25%: Scale ads, expand to more cities

## Alternative: Hybrid Approach (New to City + Open to Locals)

**Problem with Pure Niche**: May limit initial user base too much

**Solution**: 
- Primary niche = "New to City" (3-6 months)
- Secondary option = "I want to meet new arrivals" (locals who like showing city)

**Why this works:**
- Increases supply (locals join to meet newcomers)
- Maintains niche focus (app is ABOUT new arrivals, not generic dating)
- Viral loop: Locals bring their newcomer friends

**Paywall still niche-focused:**
- Free: See 3 "new to city" matches
- Premium: See all newcomers + chat unlimited
